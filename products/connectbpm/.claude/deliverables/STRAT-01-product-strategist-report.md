# STRAT-01 — Product Strategist Report (ConnectBPM)

**Deliverable**: `products/connectbpm/docs/strategy/OPPORTUNITY-connectbpm.md` — read that for the full analysis, sources and diagrams.
**Research notes**: `products/connectbpm/.claude/scratch/STRAT-01-research-notes.md`
**Status**: Complete · For CEO review · 2026-08-20

1. **Market**: global BPM software TAM ≈ USD 23B (2026), 14–20% CAGR — Grand View, Precedence, Fortune BI. Gartner's first BOAT Magic Quadrant (Oct 2025) is dissolving "BPM suite" as a standalone category.
2. **SAM ≈ USD 400M/yr**, built bottom-up: ~36,000 GCC organisations × USD 11K blended ACV. **SOM ≈ USD 5.5M ARR at month 36** (~1.4% of SAM).
3. **The defining force is substitutes, not rivalry.** Power Automate is bundled free into every M365 business plan; Premium is USD 15/user/mo. The price floor in this market is zero.
4. **AI process authoring is NOT a wedge.** Microsoft's Copilot Studio Agentic Workflow Builder went GA 20 May 2026; n8n ships an AI Workflow Builder. Natural-language-to-workflow is a 24-week moat.
5. **SWOT verdict**: strengths are build-side (`@connectsw/*` packages, Arabic/RTL proven in `connectin`/`muaththir`, `credit-os` workflow IP, AI-native velocity). Weaknesses are sell-side — **zero brand, zero enterprise distribution, and no SOC 2 / ISO 27001**, which is a disqualification, not a disadvantage, in GCC procurement.
6. **RECOMMENDED WEDGE — compliance-grade process automation for the GCC: Arabic-first, in-region, audit-evidence-native.** (WD-2 ∩ WD-3, weighted score 4.05 / 3.85 vs 3.15 for AI authoring and 3.25 for OEM.)
7. **Defensible for 24 months** via four compounding layers: language (3–6mo copy time) → residency (12–18mo, a legal gate) → **evidence architecture (18–24mo — a day-one architectural commitment, not a feature)** → localised regulatory template library (compounds indefinitely).
8. **RECOMMENDED PRICING METRIC — completed process instances, never seats.** Per-seat pricing puts us in a losing USD 15/user comparison with Power Automate and suppresses the participation that creates switching cost.
9. **Tiers**: Sandbox free (1 process / 250 instances) · Starter USD 299/mo · Growth USD 899/mo · Business USD 2,499/mo · Sovereign from USD 60K/yr · overage USD 0.04/instance. **Task participants unlimited and free on every paid tier.** AI authoring metered separately.
10. **HARD PRODUCT REQUIREMENT — 13 engine-level meters (M1–M13)**, §7.3 of the assessment. Most critical: **M3** designer-vs-participant role separation (or the free-participant entitlement leaks), **M7** sandbox CPU/memory per tenant (COGS *and* the security boundary), **M12** idempotent replayable exactly-once accounting on an at-least-once substrate, **M13** pre-start quota refusal hook. None can be retrofitted.
11. **RECOMMENDED PORTFOLIO PLAY — compounding, not standalone.** External multi-tenant product with a clean internal API boundary from day one; `connectgrc` as first internal design partner (control testing, evidence collection, remediation, attestation are all long-running processes) — but in the **Next** horizon, never as a Now-horizon dependency.
12. **`credit-os` overlap must be arbitrated by ADR in ARCH-01**: ConnectBPM owns the generic engine; `credit-os` keeps credit domain semantics; harvest patterns, do not fork code; no migration before month 24.
13. **Now (0–6mo)**: designer, engine with exactly-once semantics, forms, task inbox, analytics v1, tenant isolation + expression sandboxing, definition versioning, metering M1–M13, **Arabic-first RTL from day one**, 5 GCC regulatory templates, self-serve billing, SOC 2 readiness. Explicitly excluded: AI authoring, evidence export, in-region hosting, `connectgrc` integration, SSO, connectors, agents.
14. **Next (6–18mo)**: evidence layer + export, Arabic AI authoring, in-region deployment and Sovereign tier, `connectgrc` integration, SSO/SAML/SCIM, SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001, KSA/UAE SI channel.
15. **Future (18–36mo)**: agentic orchestration with governed HITL, embeddable/OEM engine, partner template marketplace, process intelligence, sovereign on-prem edition.
16. **Success criteria** — M6: 40 activated tenants, 12 paying, USD 9K MRR, zero cross-tenant leaks, zero lost/duplicated work. M18: 90 paying + 3 Sovereign, USD 1.1M ARR, NRR ≥110%, SOC 2 Type II. M36: 450 paying + 12 Enterprise, USD 5.5M ARR, NRR ≥120%, ≥30% of ARR in-region.
17. **Most fragile assumption (A6)**: 40 paying tenants in year 1 with no sales team. Next most fragile: A9 (auditors accept engine-produced evidence) and A4 (in-region hosting feasible within 18 months). All labelled with validation routes.
18. **Kill criteria K0–K8 are defined and gated at months 3, 6, 9, 12 and 18.** K0 is the cheapest: no named design partner or ≥10/15 discovery interviews failing to confirm the wedge → **stop before significant build**. K6 (a production cross-tenant leak) triggers an immediate sales stop and a CEO decision on whether ConnectSW should operate a multi-tenant execution platform at all.
19. **90-day validation plan required before wedge-specific engineering**: 15 GCC discovery interviews, one auditor conversation on acceptable evidence, DevOps costing for in-region hosting, hands-on evaluation of Power Automate/Pipefy/n8n, and one named design partner.
20. **Overall recommendation: PURSUE, conditionally, gated at the month-3 K0 checkpoint.** The market is hostile to a generic entrant; it is open only where a regulatory or linguistic gate excludes the substitutes. Build for that gate or do not build.
